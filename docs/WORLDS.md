# The 14 worlds: original art direction

The creative brief each world was built from (moved out of `js/theme-registry.js` because it isn't needed at runtime).

| # | World (`id`) | Art direction |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Cyberpunk (`cyberpunk`) | Cyberpunk style, neon-lit futuristic city street at night, rain reflections, holographic signs, electric pink and blue lighting, dense urban atmosphere, high-tech mood |
| 02 | Art Deco (`artdeco`) | Art Deco, geometric symmetry, bold colours, streamlined luxury, metallic accents, decorative forms |
| 03 | Cave Drawings (`cave`) | Cave painting, ochre and charcoal, primitive animal figures, rough rock surface, ancient markings |
| 04 | Mosaic (`mosaic`) | Mosaic, small coloured glass tiles, tessellated image, grouted texture, shimmering fragments |
| 05 | Japanese Scroll (`ukiyo`) | Ukiyo-e, Japanese woodblock print, flat composition, vibrant colour, elegant flowing linework |
| 06 | Baroque (`baroque`) | Baroque painting style, dramatic Caravaggio lighting, intense shadows, rich textures, theatrical composition, opulent and emotional |
| 07 | Street Art (`street`) | Street art style, spray paint texture, stencil technique, bold graphics, concrete wall background, urban culture energy |
| 08 | Vaporwave (`vapor`) | Vaporwave aesthetic, pink and cyan palette, retro sunset gradient, glitch effects, classical statue element, grid pattern, nostalgic digital |
| 09 | Brutalist (`brutalist`) | Brutalist aesthetic, raw concrete textures, harsh contrasts, industrial materials, structural honesty, unpolished and powerful |
| 10 | Stained Glass (`stained`) | Stained glass style, bold black lead lines, jewel-tone glass panes, light glowing through from behind, symmetrical decorative composition, luminous colors |
| 11 | Double Exposure (`double`) | Double exposure photography, portrait merged with forest landscape, ghostly transparent overlap, conceptual fine art, ethereal quality |
| 12 | Tilt Shift (`tiltshift`) | Tilt-shift photography, miniature effect, selective focus band, slightly oversaturated colors, aerial view of city, toy-like appearance |
| 13 | Glitch Art (`glitch`) | Glitch art, digital corruption effects, RGB channel separation, pixel sorting, data bending aesthetic, neon colors on dark background |
| 14 | Frutiger Aero (`aero`) | Frutiger Aero: glossy glass and aqua surfaces, skies and clouds, bubbles and water droplets, lush green nature, lens flares and light rays, optimistic mid-2000s clean-tech aesthetic |
